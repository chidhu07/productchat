Questions

How much does Acme Notes cost?
Free for one device. Sync across all your devices is $4 a month or $36 a year.
Can you read my notes?
No. Notes are encrypted on your device before they sync, and only you hold the key.
Which devices does it run on?
Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android, plus a web app.
Can I import from Evernote or Notion?
Yes. Go to Settings → Import and pick the export file. Images and attachments come along.
How do I cancel?
Settings → Billing → Cancel. You keep sync until the end of the period you paid for.
